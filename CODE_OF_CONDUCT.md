# Ground Rules

> [!NOTE]
> Heads up: This is NOT Contributor Covenant. Read the following with care. We have more specific rules.

## The Rules

- **Don't be a jerk**. Richardery will not be tolerated.
- **Behave civilly**, and **be considerate** of your words and actions.
- No hate speech, stereotyping, discrimination, personal or characterisation attacks.
- Keep the communications and contents **appropriate and safe for work**.
  - Please do not use profanity in source code, commit messages, discussions or anywhere else.
  - No nastiness, including inappropriate intimacy/sexualisation.
- No importing or exporting drama.
- **No discussion of politics**, espically that of United States of America.
- Do not impersonate another person, user, human being, or anything which have an identity that is not yours.
- All other rules are subordinate to these.

### Other specific rules

- **No bump, filler or other low-effort comments.**
  - If a comment or post in the repository discussions matters to you, [upvote the post or comment](https://docs.github.com/en/discussions/collaborating-with-your-community-using-discussions/participating-in-a-discussion#upvoting-a-discussion).
  - If there is an issue you care about, add an ⬆️ (_arrow up_) reaction.
- **Respect and follow the [Generative AI Policy](AI_POLICY.md).**
  - Allowing your AI agent to generate pull requests, their descriptions or to answer questions in pull requests ("speak for you") is **grounds for suspension**.
  - You _can_ use your agent to generate code but you must always write the pull request description and answer questions yourself.
  - Look at [this article](https://roe.dev/blog/using-ai-in-open-source) for details.
  
## Reporting violations

- **To report a possible violation on GitHub, use the built in feature to report an issue to maintainers**. 
  - Describe the issue clearly in the description.
  - Provide a link to the content in question.

## Enforcement

The kind of enforcement depends on the previous violation history of the user in question, as well as the severity of the violation in question. In addition, if any rules says so, the violations are dealt with suspension.

Repeat offenders may get more severe enforcements if moderators believe that they aren't getting the message.

- **The first kind of enforcement is Warning**.
  - Warning is a private or public warning message sent to a violator. This can be an informal reminder, a full-on cease and desist notice, or anything in-between.
  - The relevant content is also collapsed or deleted.
- **In between, there is Suspension**.
  - If a user is suspended, they are blocked from this namespace. They cannot create new issues, pull requests, discussion post, or comment.
  - This is done when moderators believe that either the user isn't getting the message, or an immediate stop of their actions is required.
  - To have the suspension lifted, they must appeal the suspension and _politely_ show understanding of what they did wrong, and promise the problem won't happen again.
- **Finally, there is the Permanent Ban**.
  - This is the point of no return. A permanently banned user is blocked from this namespace for good.
  - Permanent bans are reserved for the most severe cases.
